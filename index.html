How We Train

The Four Pillars

Every program at Camp Strong is built on the same four pillars — the foundation of athletic performance at the highest level.

Strength Development

Strength is the foundation of all athletic performance.

  • Force production
  • Structural balance
  • Progressive overload
  • Movement efficiency

Speed, Agility & Acceleration

Speed wins games.

  • First-step explosiveness
  • Linear acceleration mechanics
  • Change-of-direction efficiency
  • Sprint technique under fatigue

Power & Explosiveness

Power is strength expressed quickly.

  • Vertical & horizontal force output
  • Rate of force development
  • Reactive ability
  • Sport-specific explosiveness

Mobility & Injury Resilience

Availability is the greatest ability.

  • Joint integrity
  • Soft tissue quality
  • Range of motion under control
  • Regeneration strategies

Perform With Power
